# Autonomous UAVs for Search & Rescue

## Complete UAV SAR Platforms

1. **A Review of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles for Search and Rescue Operations** (2021)
   - Focus: Comprehensive review of UAV technologies, sensors, and algorithms for SAR
   - Relevance: Direct background for your complete system

2. **Design, Implementation, and Evaluation of an Autonomous UAV for Search and Rescue** (2022)
   - Focus: End-to-end autonomous UAV design for SAR with flight testing
   - Relevance: Similar hardware stack (Pixhawk, RPi, camera) — compare your approach

3. **A Survey on UAV-based Disaster Management Systems** (2023)
   - Focus: UAV technologies for disaster assessment, search, and communication relay
   - Relevance: Broader than just SAR; covers multiple SARAS mission profiles

## Drone Autonomy for SAR

4. **Autonomous Navigation and Mapping in Disaster Scenarios Using UAVs** (2021)
   - Focus: Autonomous flight in GPS-denied or degraded environments
   - Relevance: Backup for disaster zones where GPS is unreliable

5. **Vision-Based Autonomous Landing for Search and Rescue UAVs** (2022)
   - Focus: Computer vision for precision landing in unstructured terrain
   - Relevance: Useful for your drone's landing in disaster zones

## Multi-Domain (Air-Ground-Water) SAR

6. **Aerial-Ground Robotic Systems for Search and Rescue: A Review** (2022)
   - Focus: Heterogeneous robot teams for SAR
   - Relevance: Expand SARAS to include ground robots in future work

## Notes
- Focus on publications from IEEE Robotics, Journal of Field Robotics, ICRA, IROS
- Test flights should be documented with metrics (flight time, detection rate, false positives)
