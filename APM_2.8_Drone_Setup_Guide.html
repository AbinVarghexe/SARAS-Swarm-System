01 APM 2.8 Board Architecture & Jumper Setup

The ArduPilot Mega (APM 2.8) is an 8-bit AVR autopilot based on the ATmega2560 microprocessor with onboard MPU6000 6-DOF accelerometer/gyro and MS5611 high-precision barometric pressure sensor.

Figure 1.1: APM 2.8 Flight Controller Functional Board Topography & Pinout Configuration
1. The MAG Jumper (Internal Compass Enable)

Located immediately adjacent to the GPS and I2C sockets. When using No External GPS/Compass puck:

  • Must be SHORTED (Jumper Cap ON): Connects the onboard HMC5883L/QMC5883 magnetometer to the MCU I2C bus.
  • If missing or left open, the autopilot will raise a Compass Not Healthy error.
2. The JP1 Jumper (Power Rail Isolation)

The JP1 header connects or bridges the power planes of the INPUTS and OUTPUTS rails:

  • With Power Module (Recommended): REMOVE JP1. This separates clean 5.3V CPU power from noisy ESC inductive spikes.
  • With ESC BEC only: Place JP1 ON to route 5V from ESC to APM MCU.

02 FlySky FS-T6 to FS-R6B Binding & Radio Setup

The FlySky FS-T6 transmitter communicates using AFHDS (Automatic Frequency Hopping Digital System) 2.4GHz protocol. The FS-R6B receiver outputs independent PWM signals on 6 separate channels.

Figure 2.1: FlySky AFHDS Wireless Binding Architecture and Terminal Layout
Four-Step Receiver Binding Procedure:
  1. Insert Bind Plug: Insert the black loop bind jumper into the `BAT` / `B/VCC` port on the FS-R6B receiver.
  2. Power Receiver: Supply 5V power to any available channel (e.g. connect a 3-wire servo cable from APM Input 1). The red LED will flash rapidly (binding mode ready).
  3. Transmit Bind Signal: On the FS-T6 transmitter, hold down the circular BIND KEY on the front faceplate while sliding the power switch to ON.
  4. Solid Red Confirmation: The LED on the receiver will switch from rapid blinking to SOLID RED. Disconnect receiver power, remove the bind plug, and cycle power to confirm permanent pairing.

Channel Mapping Table (Standard Mode 2 / AETR)

CH 2 Elevator (Pitch) Right Stick (Vertical) $1000 - 2000$ Pitches drone Forward / Aft
CH 3 Throttle Left Stick (Vertical, Unsprung) $1000 - 2000$ Direct motor thrust / Climb rate

03 Receiver (FS-R6B) to APM 2.8 Physical Wiring

The APM 2.8 incorporates dedicated 3-pin headers for Inputs 1 through 8. Because the FS-R6B provides true discrete PWM pulses for each channel, connections are made directly with standard DuPont servo wires without signal converters.

Figure 3.1: Complete Schematic Wiring Harness from FlySky FS-R6B to APM 2.8 Inputs
Three-Wire Power & Signal Rail (CH 1)

Connect a complete 3-conductor female-female servo wire between CH1 on FS-R6B and INPUT 1 on APM 2.8:

  • Inner Pin (APM): White/Orange $\rightarrow$ Signal (PWM pulses)
  • Middle Pin (APM): Red $\rightarrow$ $+5\text{V}$ Power Rail
  • Outer Pin (APM): Black/Brown $\rightarrow$ System Ground

This single cable powers the internal radio receiver circuitry.

Single Signal Wires (CH 2 through CH 5)

To avoid ground loops and messy bundle clutter, connect only the top Signal pin for remaining channels:

  • CH 2 Signal $\rightarrow$ APM INPUT 2 (Elevator / Pitch)
  • CH 3 Signal $\rightarrow$ APM INPUT 3 (Throttle)
  • CH 4 Signal $\rightarrow$ APM INPUT 4 (Rudder / Yaw)
  • CH 5 Signal $\rightarrow$ APM INPUT 5 (Flight Mode Switch)

04 Quad-X Motor Layout, ESC Outputs & Propellers

ArduCopter uses the standardized Quad-X multirotor frame geometry. Motor order and rotational direction are strictly designated to balance aerodynamic gyroscopic torque.

Figure 4.1: ArduCopter Quad-X Motor Layout, Directional Spin, and APM Output Mapping
Motor Physical Frame Location Spin Direction Propeller Model APM Output Rail Header
Motor 1 Front-Right Arm Clockwise (CW) 1045R (Pusher / Reverse Pitch) OUTPUT 1 (Inner Signal, Mid 5V, Out GND)
Motor 2 Rear-Left Arm Clockwise (CW) 1045R (Pusher / Reverse Pitch) OUTPUT 2 (Inner Signal, Mid 5V, Out GND)
Motor 3 Front-Left Arm Counter-Clockwise (CCW) 1045 (Standard Direct Pitch) OUTPUT 3 (Inner Signal, Mid 5V, Out GND)
Motor 4 Rear-Right Arm Counter-Clockwise (CCW) 1045 (Standard Direct Pitch) OUTPUT 4 (Inner Signal, Mid 5V, Out GND)
Motor Rotation Reversal Rule:
BLDC brushless motors spin in accordance with three-phase magnetic commutation. If a motor turns in the wrong direction during verification, swap any two of the three motor wires connected to that electronic speed controller (ESC). Never invert DC battery inputs!

05 Mission Planner 1.3.68 Configuration & No-GPS Tuning

Mission Planner 1.3.68 provides verified, glitch-free parameter flashing and sensor tuning for the legacy ATmega2560 APM processor.

Figure 5.1: Sequential Calibration and Parameter Tuning Workflow in Mission Planner
1. Accelerometer 6-Axis Calibration

Go to INITIAL SETUP → Mandatory Hardware → Accel Calibration:

  • Click Calibrate Accel.
  • Place vehicle Level, Left, Right, Nose Down, Nose Up, and Back when requested by audio prompt.
2. Onboard Compass Live Calibration

Go to Mandatory Hardware → Compass:

  • Select APM with On-board Compass.
  • Orientation: ROTATION_NONE.
  • Click Live Calibration, rotate vehicle 360° in all spheres until complete.
Critical No-GPS Arming & Failsafe Parameters
Because no external GPS unit is connected, ArduCopter will throw PreArm: Need 3D Fix and lock the arming sequence. You MUST adjust the following two parameters in CONFIG/TUNING → Standard Parameters:
  1. ARMING_CHECK: Change from All to Skip GPS (or raw integer value 0 for bench testing).
  2. FS_THR_ENABLE: Set to Enabled Always Land (or Land). By default, failsafe triggers RTL (Return to Launch), which causes an immediate flyaway or fatal crash without GPS coordinates!

06 All-At-Once ESC Calibration & Pre-Flight Checklist

Calibrating ESC throttle endpoints ensures all four motors commence rotation simultaneously and deliver identical linear thrust curves across the throttle band.

Figure 6.1: APM All-At-Once Simultaneous ESC Calibration Sequence
Pre-Flight Safety Readiness Checklist
  • [ ] Propellers Disconnected: Keep props detached until full arming and motor rotation tests succeed.
  • [ ] Arming Sequence: Turn on FS-T6, select STABILIZE mode, pull throttle stick down and hold FULL RIGHT for 5 seconds until the red APM arming LED turns solid.
  • [ ] Disarming Sequence: Pull throttle stick down and hold FULL LEFT for 2 seconds.
  • [ ] Vibration Dampening: Ensure APM 2.8 sits on silicone anti-vibration damping balls to prevent accelerometer saturation from frame vibrations.
  • [ ] Balance Check: Center of Gravity (CoG) must reside precisely at the geometric intersection of the two diagonal frame arms.
